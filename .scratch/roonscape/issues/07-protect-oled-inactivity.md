# 07 — Protect the OLED during inactivity

**What to build:** Reduce prolonged bright, static content without obscuring
active music or hiding useful explanations too early. Paused, stopped, and
unavailable presentations enter a restrained inactive treatment after a grace
period, while resumed playback immediately restores the full composition.

**Blocked by:** 04 — Present live Now Playing and artwork; and 05 — Make
playback and progress truthful.

### Agent Brief

**Category:** enhancement

**Summary:** Dim and reposition inactive presentations while restoring active
playback immediately.

**Current behavior:** Gallery split remains at full luminance and a fixed
position for every rendered snapshot. No inactivity clock, grace period, or
OLED-safe repositioning exists.

**Desired behavior:** Paused, stopped, and unavailable states first remain
fully legible, then enter a configurable restrained inactive treatment. Playing
always cancels that treatment and restores the normal composition immediately.

**Key interfaces:**

- Display Configuration provides grace period, dimness, and reposition cadence
  without creating a settings screen.
- Renderer state tracks time in the current playback or availability condition
  and schedules bounded layout offsets.
- State changes cancel obsolete timers and restore active luminance and
  position deterministically.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- [ ] Paused playback freezes progress and retains full presentation during
      the grace period.
- [ ] Paused, stopped, pairing-required, disconnected, and output-unavailable
      presentations dim and reposition only after explaining their state.
- [ ] Repositioning remains within safe Gallery split bounds.
- [ ] Returning to Playing immediately restores full luminance and the normal
      position.
- [ ] Deterministic tests cover grace periods, repeated repositioning, state
      changes during inactivity, and active restoration.

**Out of scope:**

- Physical OLED calibration, which belongs to Reference Deployment acceptance.
- Television power/input control or perpetual decorative motion.
