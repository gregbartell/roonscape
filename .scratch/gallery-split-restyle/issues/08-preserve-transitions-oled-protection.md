### Agent Brief

**Category:** enhancement

**Summary:** Preserve coordinated transitions, stale-content safety, and OLED
protection across every restyled presentation.

**Current behavior:** `PresentationState`, `PresentationTransition`, and the
native view already distinguish progress-only updates, immediately clear stale
content on disconnect, crossfade visual revisions, retain only current plus one
outgoing presentation, and dim/reposition inactive states within fixed bounds.
The restyled Gallery split, full-field states, larger artwork, and light
palettes do not exist yet and therefore are not covered by those guarantees.

**Desired behavior:** The same behavioral guarantees apply to every new visual
state and semantic palette role. A visual revision replaces artwork, text, and
the complete color system as one bounded transition; progress-only and
same-track updates remain in place; OLED transforms never clip the larger
composition or diagnostics.

**Key interfaces:**

- `PresentationState::update()` continues to classify progress-only,
  immediate, and transitioning changes from complete snapshots.
- `PresentationTransition` owns at most current and one outgoing presentation
  and releases the outgoing resources after its bounded duration.
- The native presentation view applies semantic palette styles to both layers
  during a crossfade and applies inactivity transforms to the complete stack.
- Inactivity policy retains configurable grace, opacity, cadence, and safe
  bounds for Paused, Idle, and unavailable conditions.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- [x] Every visual-content or palette change crossfades the whole presentation
      once, while progress-value-only updates and same-track updates do not.
- [x] Disconnect and unavailable updates immediately remove stale Now Playing
      content.
- [x] Rapid revisions remain bounded to current plus one outgoing view and
      artwork resource.
- [x] Existing inactivity semantics remain intact, Playing restores
      immediately, and every restyled element stays within safe offset bounds.
- [x] Tests exercise these guarantees for Gallery split, full-field, light,
      and missing-content states without adding motion categories.

**Out of scope:**

- New ambient animation or changes to the configured inactivity policy.
- Preserving stale content for visual continuity.
